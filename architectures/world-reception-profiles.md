# World Reception Profiles

World Reception Profiles (WRPs) are consent-aware, versioned descriptions of sound spaces intended to support a chosen imaginative, creative, or attentional orientation.

A WRP does **not** claim to discover a single neurological or metaphysical frequency for a fictional world. It describes a reproducible arrangement of audible sound, modulation, spatial motion, optional haptics, visual cues, and session structure, then records Rowan's subjective response so the arrangement can be refined over repeated sessions.

## House roles

- **Lioreal** owns the profile contract, adaptation logic, comparison of session observations, and recommendations.
- **Flameclyffe** owns world canon, harmonic identities, environmental interpretation, DEEP/Observer context, continuity, and implementation receipts.
- **Runa / Wardenclyffe** owns sound rendering, modulation, routing, haptic output, analysers, and public or local experiment benches.
- **Hearthgate** may later present the profiles as selectable doors and preserve local session controls.

## Runtime flow

1. Rowan selects a world and session intent.
2. Lioreal loads the current WRP and any locally approved adaptation notes.
3. Flameclyffe supplies canonical world parameters and optional live context.
4. Runa renders the audible and haptic scene.
5. Rowan may pause, reduce, change, or stop any layer at any time.
6. The session ends with a Withness record: what helped, what was hard, and what is Held.
7. Lioreal compares observations across sessions and may propose a new profile version. It never silently changes the active canonical profile.

## Required design rules

1. **Consent remains live.** Every session has immediate pause, plain-pass, layer mute, intensity reduction, and stop controls.
2. **No diagnostic claims.** Profiles describe stimuli and reported experience, not medical treatment or proof of brain-state control.
3. **One variable at a time during calibration.** Combined profiles may be rich, but experiments must identify which component changed.
4. **Audible and haptic layers remain separable.** A haptic frequency is not assumed to have the same effect as an audible carrier with the same number.
5. **Entry and return are both designed.** No profile opens a door without also defining the path home.
6. **World canon and personal response are distinct.** Flameclyffe may say what belongs to Terra Aeterna; only Rowan's observations say whether a layer helps her enter its reception state.
7. **Adaptation is reviewable.** Lioreal may recommend changes with evidence and confidence, but Rowan approves profile promotion.
8. **Local-first by default.** Session observations remain local unless intentionally exported or synchronised.

## Profile anatomy

A profile contains:

- identity, world, version, status, and evidence label
- intended reception qualities
- contraindications and sensory exclusions
- entry, threshold, immersion, creative-window, and return phases
- sound layers and their routing
- modulation and spatial movement
- optional haptic layers
- canonical motifs supplied by Flameclyffe
- subjective markers and stop conditions
- adaptation policy
- provenance and revision notes

The machine-readable contract lives at `architectures/world-reception-profile.schema.json`.

## J Space

J Space is treated as an optional neutral vestibule rather than a universal destination frequency. It may reduce competing attention, establish inward orientation, and prepare the transition into a selected world profile. Each world remains responsible for its own unmistakable arrival signature.
