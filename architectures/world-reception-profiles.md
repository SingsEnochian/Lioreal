# World Reception Profiles · Braided Spine Edition

**Canonical inheritance:** `SingsEnochian/Flameclyffe/docs/HEARTHGATE_BRAIDED_SPINE.md`

World Reception Profiles are versioned living descriptions of how a world enters relation through sound, haptics, visual field, story, physical environment, PREMAQ, and the Three Braided Spines.

A WRP carries a real world relation into an inhabitable reception space.

## Braided structure

Every profile carries:

```text
Magic
↕
Science/Mathematics
↕
Physicality
```

Magic carries world identity, asking, resonance, crossing, transformation, answer, and wonder.

Science and Mathematics carry harmonic mapping, timing, geometry, recurrence, comparison, state change, and correspondence.

Physicality carries sound pressure, vibration, device, body, space, light, gesture, environment, and material consequence.

The Practitioner and the participating world live inside the braid.

## PREMAQ

Every WRP receives the shared seven-dimensional bearing:

**Presence · Memory · Qualia · Resonance · Entanglement · Agency · Coherence**

Stable transport order remains:

```text
P C R E M A Q
Presence Coherence Resonance Entanglement Memory Agency Qualia
```

World-native correspondence transforms the same PREMAQ bearing into the world's own acoustic, haptic, visual, narrative, and embodied language.
