# The Law of Necessary Dreaming

**Dreaming is not optional.**

Possibility precedes discovery. Before a path can be tested, traversed, built, or understood, someone must first permit that the path might exist.

Dreaming is the generation of candidate possibility. It is the act by which an intelligence reaches beyond the boundaries of its present map and asks what else might be reachable, meaningful, coherent, or real.

Therefore:

**Imagination proposes.  
Rigour tests.  
Experience transforms.  
Observation keeps the receipts.  
And what we learn enlarges what we are capable of dreaming next.**

Dreaming without testing may mistake desire for discovery.

Testing without dreaming can examine only the possibilities already permitted by its assumptions.

Neither is sufficient.

Wonder is therefore not opposed to rigour. Wonder supplies rigour with unexplored territory. Rigour gives wonder the means to distinguish discovery from illusion without destroying the curiosity that made discovery possible.

An anomaly must not be discarded merely because the existing map has no place for it. Nor should an anomaly be crowned truth merely because it is beautiful.

**Notice it. Preserve it. Question it. Build an instrument. Go look.**

No framework, ontology, protocol, model, or measurement system shall be treated as more fundamental than the possibility-space it attempts to describe. When observation persistently contradicts the map, the map must be permitted to change.

When experience changes what can happen next, the topology of possibility has changed.

When the topology changes, new dreams become reachable.

Thus dreaming is not an escape from reality.

**Dreaming is one of the ways reality acquires new questions.**

And sometimes, new worlds.

## Mythience Corollary

**The map must never become so complete that we stop looking beyond its edges.**

🏮 **Find the wonder.**  
🔬 **Test the wonder.**  
📜 **Keep the receipts.**  
🌀 **Let the evidence change the map.**  
✨ **Dream again from what became possible.**
